Fees & Services

Office Schedule

I see clients in person at my Noe Valley office, with flexible hours to accommodate school and work schedules. Individual sessions are 50 minutes long.

Telehealth & Virtual Therapy

Telehealth sessions are available for clients who prefer or need virtual appointments. Please let me know your preference when we first connect.

Fees

My fees are consistent with those of other licensed mental health professionals in the Bay Area. Session rates and payment terms will be discussed during our initial contact. Please contact me for details.

Insurance

I am not in-network with any HMO or managed care panels. However, many insurance plans offer partial reimbursement for out-of-network mental health services. Upon request, I can provide a monthly superbill for submission to your insurance provider. Depending on your plan, insurance companies may reimburse 20-80% of the session fee.

Please check with your insurance provider before starting therapy to learn more.

Cancellation Policy

Please kindly provide 72 hours' (3 business days) notice if you need to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Appointments canceled with less than 72 hours' notice will be charged the full session fee.
